Welcome to Oaza

Oaza is Macedonian for oasis — and that is exactly what we set out to be: a calm, well-kept retreat in the middle of Štip, minutes from the old town, the Bezisten gallery and the climb to Isar Fortress.

Behind the faceted terracotta facade you'll find a hotel run with genuine Macedonian hospitality: a reception that never closes, a kitchen that cooks the way this region likes to eat, and a team happy to help in Macedonian or English.

The city

Štip, on foot

One of Macedonia's oldest towns, Štip wraps around the hill of Isar Fortress. From our door, the old town, galleries, cafés and the university are all within easy reach — and there's pastrmajlija, the city's famous dish, to try.

  1. Isar Fortress

    Medieval hilltop fortress above the city, with sweeping views over the Bregalnica valley.

  2. Bezisten

    An Ottoman-era covered market, today the city's art gallery.

  3. Novo Selo

    Štip's old quarter of 19th-century townhouses and churches.

  4. Kežovica Spa

    Thermal mineral springs and baths on the edge of town.

  5. Pastrmajlija

    Štip's signature dish — an oval flatbread topped with cured meat, celebrated every year with its own festival.
